    What this study represents

    Real coastlines and ETOPO-derived elevation. The coarse elevation grid shows major mountain ranges, with adjustable vertical exaggeration; individual peaks and small islands are simplified. A footprint is an approximate area around a place you visited. Country borders do not decide what lights up, and no travel lines are inferred.

    Raised terrain stays fixed as your lighted areas grow. This is a visual and geometry study; the simulator is not connected to hardware. STL files contain shape only, with no color or light.
