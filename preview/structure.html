Dimensions and model assumptions

Sea-level radius 152.5 mm; smooth shell underside 151.5 mm. Relief is the same sampled geographic elevation at 25× used in the simulator. The radial base wall is 1 mm; mountain areas are thicker.

The 12 outer sections and 320 triangular carriers are a packaging study, not a final part count or manufacturing layout. Facets around the south pole are omitted to clear the support spine. Full-globe LED dots are representative; they do not show the required 2–2.5 mm final pitch. Complete optical cells appear only in the small prototype view.

Rings and ribs represent the intended load path, but carrier brackets and shell clips are not yet modeled. The base is a 200 × 30 mm envelope; weight and stability require the finished mass. The prototype's 13 × 9 active array is nominal 39 × 27 mm at 3 mm pitch, on a 39 × 51.08 mm reference board. Chip/connector shapes and heights are approximate.

Colors distinguish components. The actual baffles should be opaque black; green boards and gray baffles would be hidden in the finished object.
